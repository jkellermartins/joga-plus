What Is Footvolley?

Footvolley is a Brazilian sport that combines elements of soccer and beach volleyball. Players use their feet, chest, and head to volley a ball over a net on a sand court. It builds full-body coordination, explosive power, and creative instincts that transfer directly to soccer, volleyball, and every other sport. Joga+ is the only academy in the DMV offering structured footvolley training with CBFV-certified coaches who competed on Brazil's national circuit.

Who Is Footvolley Training For?

Anyone ages 8 and up — no experience required. Soccer players looking to develop superior touch and body control. Volleyball athletes who want sand training that improves their indoor game. Multi-sport athletes seeking explosive power and coordination. Complete beginners who want to learn a new sport. We serve athletes across Washington DC, Northern Virginia (Arlington, McLean, Falls Church, Fairfax), and Maryland (Bethesda, Rockville, Silver Spring).

Footvolley Training FAQ

Common Questions.

What is footvolley?

Footvolley is a Brazilian sport that combines soccer skills with beach volleyball rules. Players use their feet, chest, and head to volley a ball over a net on sand. Footvolley training builds explosive power, full-body coordination, and creative touch that transfers directly to soccer and other sports. Joga+ is the only academy in the DMV with CBFV-certified Brazilian footvolley coaches. No experience needed — beginners rally within their first sessions.

Do I need experience to start footvolley?

No. Many Joga+ footvolley athletes start with zero experience. Our coaches assess every athlete individually and build a development plan that matches their current level. Beginners rally within their first few sessions. Already competing? We film, analyze, and rebuild your game for tournament-level play.

Can footvolley help my soccer or volleyball game?
