# JOGA+ Ebook Production Workflow

> The complete repeatable system for producing every JOGA+ digital training product.
> Follow stages in order. Each stage has a clear output before moving on.
> Clinical. Organized. Repeatable.

---

## Workflow Overview

Idea → Audience → Skill → Offer → Outline → Methodology → Drills → Weekly Plans → Parent Section → Tracker → Video → Design → Export → Upload → Payment → Preview → Mockups → Reels → Carousels → DM/Email → Ads → Launch → Iterate.

25 stages. Every product. Every time.

---

## Stage 1 — Product Idea

**Goal:** Name the product and the problem it solves.

- One-sentence product description.
- The painful problem it solves for the athlete or parent.
- Why JOGA+ is the right brand to solve it.
- Working title.

**Output:** A single-page concept brief.

---

## Stage 2 — Audience Definition

**Goal:** Lock the buyer and the user.

- Buyer: who pays (usually a parent).
- User: who trains (the athlete).
- Age group.
- Skill level: Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced.
- Sport or focus area.
- Where they train (home, court, field, small space).
- What they already tried that didn't work.

**Output:** Audience card.

---

## Stage 3 — Skill Focus

**Goal:** Define the single skill or system the ebook builds.

- Primary skill (the headline).
- Secondary skills (supporting outcomes).
- The transformation: from → to.
- The promise: what the athlete can do after finishing.

**Output:** Skill transformation statement.

---

## Stage 4 — Offer Positioning

**Goal:** Position the ebook in the JOGA+ product ladder.

- Price tier (free, low-ticket, bundle, premium).
- What it replaces (a private lesson, a clinic, a camp).
- What it leads to (the next product up the ladder).
- The hook: the one sentence that sells it.

**Output:** Offer one-pager.

---

## Stage 5 — Ebook Outline

**Goal:** Lock the full table of contents using `master-ebook-template.md`.

- Section-by-section outline.
- Word count target per section.
- Page count estimate.
- Program length (weeks).
- Drill count target.

**Output:** Final outline approved before any writing begins.

---

## Stage 6 — Training Methodology

**Goal:** Write the methodology section.

- Confirm the six JOGA+ pillars.
- Adapt language to the sport and age.
- Explain the *why* behind the structure.
- Keep it under 600 words. This is the manifesto.

**Output:** Methodology section, final copy.

---

## Stage 7 — Drill Writing

**Goal:** Write every drill using `drill-template.md`.

- Build the full drill library first.
- Group drills by category.
- Confirm each drill has: setup, instructions, coaching points, common mistakes, regression, progression, challenge, parent/coach note, QR slot.
- Peer review each drill out loud before locking.

**Output:** Complete drill library document.

---

## Stage 8 — Weekly Plan Creation

**Goal:** Map drills into weekly plans using `weekly-plan-template.md`.

- Select variant: 3 / 4 / 5 days per week.
- Assign weekly themes.
- Place drills inside daily slots.
- Define weekly challenges.
- Write reflection questions.

**Output:** Full multi-week training calendar.

---

## Stage 9 — Parent Education Section

**Goal:** Adapt `parent-guide-template.md` to the product.

- Tailor parent guidance to the sport and age.
- Add 3–5 product-specific tips.
- Rewrite sample questions to match the program.

**Output:** Final parent section.

---

## Stage 10 — Progress Tracker Creation

**Goal:** Build the tracker pages.

- One page per week (or weekly spread).
- Checkboxes for sessions.
- Self-rating block.
- Improvement prompts.
- Signature line.

**Output:** Printable progress tracker.

---

## Stage 11 — QR Video Planning

**Goal:** Map every drill, warm-up, and challenge to a video.

- Master video list with: drill name, duration, coach, status.
- Generate QR slots in the layout (real QRs come post-recording).
- Plan a master playlist QR for the front of the book.

**Output:** Video shot list.

---

## Stage 12 — Video Recording Checklist

**Goal:** Record every video to a consistent standard.

- Single camera angle, locked tripod.
- Black or neutral background.
- Coach in branded JOGA+ kit.
- 15–45 seconds per drill.
- One demonstration per video, slow then fast.
- No music. Add in post.
- Title card + outro frame: black with neon `+`.
- File naming: `JP-[SPORT]-[DRILL#]-[NAME].mp4`.

**Output:** Raw footage organized in one folder per product.

---

## Stage 13 — Canva Design Layout

**Goal:** Produce the visual layout for non-technical edits.

- Use the JOGA+ master Canva template.
- Apply the rules in `ebook-style-guide.md`.
- Build cover, dividers, drill pages, weekly plans, parent pages, tracker, CTA.
- Insert real QR codes once videos are live.

**Output:** Finished Canva file, version-locked.

---

## Stage 14 — HTML/CSS Layout Option

**Goal:** Optional premium typesetting using HTML + Paged.js or print CSS.

- Use brand tokens: `#050505 / #FFFFFF / #39FF14 / #111111 / #F4F4F4`.
- Space Grotesk + Inter loaded from a single font source.
- Print CSS for PDF export (A4 + US Letter).
- One stylesheet per ebook, extending a base stylesheet.

**Output:** Web-rendered ebook source for high-fidelity exports.

---

## Stage 15 — PDF Export

**Goal:** Final, production-grade PDF.

- 300dpi for print, 2x retina for digital.
- Embedded fonts.
- Hyperlinks active (QR + CTA URLs).
- File size optimized (target under 25MB).
- Filename: `JOGAplus-[Product]-v[Version].pdf`.
- QA pass: every QR scanned on a real phone.

**Output:** Final PDF master + a low-res preview PDF.

---

## Stage 16 — Website Upload

**Goal:** Publish the product page on `jogaplusacademy.com`.

- Product page with cover mockup, transformation, contents, sample pages, FAQ.
- Buy button.
- Delivery method (instant download or email).
- Mobile-optimized layout.
- Brand-consistent visuals only.

**Output:** Live product page.

---

## Stage 17 — Stripe Connection

**Goal:** Connect payment to delivery.

- Stripe payment link or embedded checkout.
- Success page → instant download or auto-email with PDF link.
- Receipt email branded JOGA+.
- Refund policy linked.
- Test with a real card. Refund the test.

**Output:** Working purchase + delivery flow.

---

## Stage 18 — Free Preview Version

**Goal:** Build a teaser PDF.

- 6–10 pages.
- Includes: cover, methodology, one drill, one weekly plan day, parent intro, CTA.
- Watermark: *"Free Preview — Full Edition at jogaplusacademy.com"* (subtle).
- Gated by email capture on the site.

**Output:** Lead-magnet PDF + email capture flow.

---

## Stage 19 — Product Images / Mockups

**Goal:** Generate sales visuals.

- Flat cover mockup.
- Tablet mockup (athlete-friendly).
- Phone mockup (mobile-first reading).
- Spread mockup (open ebook showing drill page).
- Lifestyle mockup (athlete holding tablet on court/field — keep generic, no signage).

**Output:** Mockup pack at 2400px and 1080px.

---

## Stage 20 — Instagram Reel Content

**Goal:** 5–10 reels to launch the ebook.

- Reel 1: Hook — *"The drill 90% of players skip."*
- Reel 2: Behind the scenes of the ebook design.
- Reel 3: One drill from the book demonstrated.
- Reel 4: Parent education clip.
- Reel 5: Transformation story.
- Reel 6: Coach POV — why the system works.
- Reel 7: Common mistake breakdown.
- Reel 8: Day-in-the-life of a JOGA+ athlete.
- Reel 9: Direct ebook walkthrough.
- Reel 10: Final CTA reel.

**Output:** Reel shot list + captions + hashtag set.

---

## Stage 21 — Instagram Carousel Content

**Goal:** 5–10 carousels.

- Carousel 1: 5 things every [sport] player must master.
- Carousel 2: Weekly training plan teaser.
- Carousel 3: The JOGA+ methodology pillars.
- Carousel 4: Parent do's and don'ts.
- Carousel 5: Drill of the week.
- Carousel 6: Before/after athlete development principles.
- Carousel 7: Inside the ebook (cover → drill → tracker).
- Carousel 8: Testimonials.
- Carousel 9: FAQ.
- Carousel 10: Launch announcement.

**Output:** Carousel design pack + caption pack.

---

## Stage 22 — Email / DM Promotion

**Goal:** Convert the warm list first.

- Pre-launch teaser email (Day -7).
- Launch email (Day 0).
- Bonus / urgency email (Day 3).
- Last call email (Day 7).
- DM script for past inquiries.
- DM script for parents who downloaded the free preview.

**Output:** Email sequence + DM scripts.

---

## Stage 23 — Ad Creative

**Goal:** Paid-ready creative for IG, TikTok, YouTube Shorts.

- 3 hook variations.
- 3 visual styles (drill clip, parent voiceover, athlete transformation).
- 3 calls to action.
- 9-frame creative matrix for testing.
- Compliant copy — no medical or guaranteed-outcome claims.

**Output:** Ad creative pack.

---

## Stage 24 — Launch Checklist

**Goal:** Single checklist to flip the switch.

- [ ] Final PDF uploaded
- [ ] Stripe flow tested with real card
- [ ] Product page live and QA'd on mobile
- [ ] Free preview delivering automatically
- [ ] Reels scheduled
- [ ] Carousels scheduled
- [ ] Email sequence queued
- [ ] DM scripts loaded
- [ ] Ad creative uploaded (paused, ready)
- [ ] Analytics tracking confirmed
- [ ] Refund + support inbox monitored
- [ ] Backup of all source files saved

**Output:** Signed-off launch checklist.

---

## Stage 25 — Post-Launch Improvements

**Goal:** Convert the first 30 days into the next version.

- Track: conversion rate, refund rate, completion rate, top traffic source.
- Collect: 5 buyer interviews, 10 written testimonials.
- Identify: top FAQ, top objection, top compliment.
- Update: v1.1 with fixes, better mockups, sharper copy.
- Document: what to keep, what to cut, what to add to the next ebook.

**Output:** Post-launch report + v1.1 update plan.

## JOGA+ Digital Product Ladder

A clear progression of products. Each step deepens commitment and value.

| Tier | Product | Purpose | Price Range |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Free Lead Magnet** — short preview PDF or single-drill mini-guide | Capture email, build trust | Free |
| 2 | **Low-Ticket Ebook** — focused training guide on one skill or program | Entry purchase, prove value | $9–$29 |
| 3 | **Bundle** — multi-ebook pack (e.g., complete soccer fundamentals set) | Increase AOV, batch sale | $39–$79 |
| 4 | **Video Course** — drills + weekly plans + on-demand video instruction | Deepen learning, raise price ceiling | $99–$249 |
| 5 | **Membership** — monthly access to fresh content, live calls, community | Recurring revenue, retention | $19–$49 / month |
| 6 | **App Training System** — structured in-app program with tracking | Daily habit product | $9–$29 / month |
| 7 | **In-Person Academy Training** — live coaching, camps, clinics, private sessions | Highest-value transformation | $$$ |
| 8 | **Coach Education Products** — methodology licensing, certification, curriculum | B2B revenue, brand expansion | $$$$ |

**Ladder logic:**
- Every product points to the next product above it.
- Every CTA includes the next-step offer.
- Every email sequence ends with the next-step offer.
- Free → low-ticket → bundle → course → membership → app → in-person → coach education.

Build the ladder once. Sell up it forever.
