# JOGA+ Weekly Plan Template

> Reusable weekly training schedule for every JOGA+ ebook.
> Works for any sport, any age group, any program length.
> Three variants: **3 days/week**, **4 days/week**, **5 days/week**.

---

## Weekly Header (use for every week)

```
## Week [NUMBER] — [WEEKLY THEME]

**Main Objective:** [What the player will master this week]
**Sport:** [SPORT]
**Age Group:** [AGE GROUP]
**Recommended Frequency:** [3 / 4 / 5 days per week]
**Session Duration:** [20 / 30 / 45 / 60 minutes]
**Weekly Challenge:** [Name of this week's challenge]
**Reflection Question:** [One question the player answers at week's end]
```

---

## Daily Session Format (used in every variant)

```
### Day [N] — [FOCUS]

**Focus:** [Skill or theme of the day]
**Drills:** [Drill #1, Drill #2, Drill #3]
**Duration:** [Total minutes]
**Coaching Emphasis:** [The one cue that matters today]
**Challenge:** [Small daily push — reps, time, or quality target]
**Reflection:** [Quick prompt the player answers before closing the session]
**Progress Checkpoint:** ☐ Completed
```

## Variant A — 3 Days Per Week

> Ideal for: beginners, busy schedules, in-season athletes, parent education programs.

### Day 1 — Foundations
**Focus:** Technical fundamentals
**Drills:** Drill 1, Drill 2, Drill 3
**Duration:** 25–30 min
**Coaching Emphasis:** Clean execution. Slow is smooth, smooth is fast.
**Challenge:** Complete every set with zero broken reps.
**Reflection:** Which foot/hand/side felt weaker today?
**Progress Checkpoint:** ☐

### Day 2 — Coordination & Body Control
**Focus:** Footwork, balance, rhythm
**Drills:** Drill 4, Drill 5, Drill 6
**Duration:** 25–30 min
**Coaching Emphasis:** Light feet. Stay tall. Stay loose.
**Challenge:** No stumbles in the ladder/agility sequence.
**Reflection:** Where did you lose balance today?
**Progress Checkpoint:** ☐

### Day 3 — Creativity & Game Application
**Focus:** 1v1, creativity, decision-making
**Drills:** Drill 7, Drill 8, Drill 9 + Weekly Challenge
**Duration:** 30–35 min
**Coaching Emphasis:** Try something new. Mistakes are reps.
**Challenge:** Use a move you've never used before.
**Reflection:** What's one thing you'd repeat in a real match?
**Progress Checkpoint:** ☐

## Variant C — 5 Days Per Week

> Ideal for: serious athletes, off-season builds, academy track players, advanced level.

### Day 1 — Foundations
**Focus:** Pure technical
**Drills:** Drill 1, Drill 2, Drill 3
**Duration:** 35 min
**Coaching Emphasis:** Perfect reps only.
**Challenge:** 100 clean reps in the primary drill.
**Reflection:** What was your tempo today?
**Progress Checkpoint:** ☐

### Day 2 — Coordination & Athletic Base
**Focus:** Ladder, jumps, mobility, body control
**Drills:** Drill 4, Drill 5, Drill 6
**Duration:** 30 min
**Coaching Emphasis:** Light, sharp, balanced.
**Challenge:** Zero foot faults.
**Reflection:** What part of your body felt slow?
**Progress Checkpoint:** ☐

### Day 3 — Technical Under Pressure
**Focus:** Speed, decision-making, scanning
**Drills:** Drill 7, Drill 8, Drill 9
**Duration:** 40 min
**Coaching Emphasis:** Quick decisions. Cleaner than yesterday.
**Challenge:** Beat your decision count from last session.
**Reflection:** When did your decisions slow you down?
**Progress Checkpoint:** ☐

### Day 4 — 1v1 & Creativity
**Focus:** Moves, deception, courage
**Drills:** Drill 10, Drill 11, Drill 12
**Duration:** 35 min
**Coaching Emphasis:** Try the move. Sell the move. Finish the move.
**Challenge:** Land 5 different finishing moves in one session.
**Reflection:** Which move are you committed to mastering?
**Progress Checkpoint:** ☐

### Day 5 — Game Intelligence + Weekly Challenge
**Focus:** Reading the game, weekly challenge
**Drills:** Drill 13 + Weekly Challenge + light recovery work
**Duration:** 35 min
**Coaching Emphasis:** Think the game. Then play it.
**Challenge:** Complete the weekly challenge to recorded standard.
**Reflection:** What did you learn about yourself this week?
**Progress Checkpoint:** ☐
