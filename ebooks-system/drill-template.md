## Example Drill (Reference Only)

## Drill 01: Inside–Outside Foundation

**Objective:** Build clean, repeatable ball control using both surfaces of the foot.
**Sport:** Soccer
**Age Group:** 9–14
**Difficulty:** Beginner
**Equipment:** 1 ball
**Space Needed:** 3 ft × 3 ft

### Setup

Player stands with ball at feet, shoulders square, knees soft, weight on the balls of the feet.

### Instructions

1. Touch the ball with the **inside** of the right foot.
2. Touch the ball with the **outside** of the right foot.
3. Repeat for 30 seconds on the right foot.
4. Switch to the left foot for 30 seconds.
5. Alternate feet (inside-out right, inside-out left) for 30 seconds.

### Duration

3 rounds × 90 seconds. 30 seconds rest between rounds.

### Coaching Points

- Stay on the balls of your feet.
- Light, quick touches — don't push the ball.
- Eyes up between touches.
- Keep hips loose.
- Equal quality on both feet.

### Common Mistakes

- Heels on the ground.
- Looking down the entire time.
- Hard, heavy touches.
- One foot dominating the rep count.

### Regression

Do it stationary at half speed for 60 seconds per foot.

### Progression

Add a cone slalom: every 10 touches, dribble around a cone and return.

### Player Challenge

Complete 100 clean alternating touches without losing control.

### Parent/Coach Note

Watch the eyes, not the feet. If the player is staring down the whole time, remind them: *"Eyes up between touches."* Don't correct technique mid-rep.
