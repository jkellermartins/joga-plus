# JOGA+ Ebook Style Guide

> The visual and editorial system for every JOGA+ digital training guide.
> Premium. Athletic. Clean. Sharp. Minimal. High-performance. Academy-level.

---

## 1. Brand Personality

**JOGA+ is:**
- Premium without being pretentious
- Athletic without being aggressive
- Modern without chasing trends
- Disciplined without being cold
- Creative without being chaotic
- Confident without being loud

**Reference points:** Nike training apps, Adidas Football editorial, Apple product pages, Equinox brand voice, Whoop performance reports.

**JOGA+ is not:**
- Childish
- Cluttered
- Overdesigned
- Cartoonish
- Generic stock-photo fitness
- A youth club newsletter

---

## 2. Typography Direction

**Display / Headlines:** Space Grotesk — weights 500, 600, 700
**Body:** Inter — weights 300, 400, 500, 600, 700, 900
**Numerals & stats:** Space Grotesk (tabular figures where possible)

**Hierarchy:**
- H1: 48–64pt · Space Grotesk 700 · uppercase optional
- H2: 32–40pt · Space Grotesk 600
- H3: 22–26pt · Space Grotesk 600
- H4: 16–18pt · Inter 700 · letter-spacing +0.04em
- Body: 15–17pt · Inter 400 · line-height 1.55–1.7
- Caption / label: 11–12pt · Inter 600 · uppercase · letter-spacing +0.08em

**Rules:**
- Never use more than 2 typefaces per page.
- Body copy: dark gray on white, or white on black. No mid-gray on mid-gray.
- Avoid italics for emphasis. Use weight instead.
- Avoid all-caps body copy. Caps reserved for labels and chips.

---

## 3. Color Palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Black | `#050505` | Primary background, hero, drill cards |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Primary background (light pages), body copy on dark |
| Neon Green | `#39FF14` | Accent only — CTAs, numbers, highlights, the `+` mark |
| Dark Gray | `#111111` | Section backgrounds, cards, dividers on black |
| Light Gray | `#F4F4F4` | Section backgrounds, drill cards on white |

**Accent discipline:**
- Neon green = **less than 10% of the page**. It's a spotlight, not wallpaper.
- Never use neon green for body text longer than one line.
- Never gradient the neon. Keep it pure.
- One accent per spread is plenty.

---

## 4. Spacing Rules

Use an **8pt grid**. All margins, padding, and gaps in multiples of 8.

- Page margin (print/PDF): 56–72pt
- Page margin (digital): 32–48px
- Section spacing: 64–96pt
- Card padding: 24–32pt
- Element gap: 8 / 16 / 24 / 32
- Line spacing: 1.55–1.7 for body

**Rule of generosity:** When in doubt, more whitespace. Premium feel comes from breathing room.

---

## 5. Page Structure

Every page follows a predictable skeleton:

1. **Page header** — section name (top-left), page number (top-right)
2. **H2 heading** — bold, with optional neon accent line beneath
3. **Lede paragraph** — one short sentence introducing the page
4. **Body content** — the meat of the page
5. **Footer** — JOGA+ wordmark (bottom-left), QR/CTA (bottom-right) where relevant

Consistency is the design. Predictable structure = premium product.

---

## 6. Visual Hierarchy

Top to bottom on every page:
1. The label (small, uppercase, neon or gray)
2. The headline (large, bold)
3. The supporting paragraph (medium, regular)
4. The action (button, QR, link)

Reader's eye should travel from **label → headline → action** in under 2 seconds.

---

## 7. Cover Page Style

- Background: full black `#050505`
- Title: Space Grotesk 700, white, 64–96pt
- Subtitle: Inter 400, light gray, 18–22pt
- JOGA+ logo: top-left, 80–120px wide
- A single bold visual element (athlete silhouette, ball graphic, abstract neon line) — never a busy photo collage
- Neon green `+` symbol used as a corner mark or chapter marker
- Bottom: edition / year in small caps

Less is more. A cover should look like a poster, not a flyer.

---

## 8. Section Divider Style

- Full-bleed black page
- Centered: large Space Grotesk numeral (the section number) in neon green
- Below: section title in white, Inter 700, uppercase
- Optional: one-line section purpose underneath in light gray

Section dividers are pauses. Let them breathe.

---

## 9. Drill Page Layout

Two-column or stacked layout:

**Top band**
- Drill number (small, uppercase, neon)
- Drill name (H2, bold)
- Sport · Age · Difficulty chips

**Left column**
- Objective
- Setup
- Instructions (numbered)

**Right column**
- Coaching points
- Common mistakes
- Regression / Progression
- Player challenge
- Parent/coach note
- QR code (bottom-right)

**Visual rule:** every drill page must include a QR slot, even if blank — keeps layout consistent across the library.

---

## 10. Weekly Plan Layout

- Week number + theme as the page header (large)
- Main objective in a neon-accented callout box
- Days listed vertically as cards (Day 1 → Day N)
- Each day card: focus, drills, duration, emphasis, challenge, reflection
- End-of-week summary as a checklist block at the bottom

Cards over tables. Cards scale better on mobile PDFs.

---

## 11. Parent Page Layout

- Softer rhythm: more whitespace, lighter accent usage
- Lead with a single bold statement at the top
- Sections numbered 1–11 (per parent guide template)
- Pull quotes in neon green for the most important reminders
- No drill graphics — this is a reading section

Parents should feel respected, not lectured.

---

## 12. Progress Tracker Layout

- One page per week, or a multi-week spread
- Checkboxes for daily sessions (large, easy to mark on screen or print)
- Self-rating bar (1–10)
- Two short prompts: improved / to work on
- Signature line at the bottom
- Neon used only on the current-week marker

Designed to be **printable, fillable, photographable.**

---

## 13. Image Usage

- Black-and-white photography preferred. Add a single neon green accent in post if needed.
- Athletes shown training, not posing.
- No stock-photo families, no thumbs-up shots, no cheering crowds.
- Crop tight. Focus on movement, feet, hands, eyes.
- Never use low-resolution images. Below 300dpi (print) or 2x retina (digital) — replace.

---

## 14. Icon Usage

- Line icons only. 1.5–2pt stroke.
- Monochrome — white on dark, black on light, neon for active state.
- Use a consistent icon set across the entire ebook (do not mix Material + Feather + custom).
- Icons support text, never replace it. Always pair with a label.

---

## 15. QR Code Placement

- Bottom-right corner of drill, warm-up, and challenge pages
- Size: 80–120px square
- Always on white card (even on black pages) for scannability
- Label below: *"Scan for video"* in caption type
- Quiet zone: never bleed text or graphics into the QR margin
- Test every QR before final export

---

## 16. CTA Page Layout

- Full-bleed black background
- One large headline (e.g., *"Train with us."*)
- One short paragraph
- One primary CTA button (neon green, black text, bold)
- One secondary link (white underline)
- Social handles at bottom, small and clean

One ask per page. Multiple CTAs kill conversion.

---

## 17. Coaching Tone

How a JOGA+ coach speaks in writing:

- **Direct.** "Stay on the balls of your feet."
- **Specific.** "30 reps. Both feet. Eyes up."
- **Encouraging.** "Cleaner than yesterday."
- **Confident.** "This works. Trust the process."
- **Honest.** "If it's easy, you're not training hard enough."

Avoid hype words: *"crushing it,"* *"beast mode,"* *"insane gains."*

---

## 18. Writing Tone

- Short sentences. Strong verbs.
- One idea per paragraph.
- Speak to the athlete, not at them.
- Use "you" — never "the player" — in athlete-facing copy.
- Use "your athlete" when speaking to parents.
- Read every paragraph out loud. If it doesn't sound like a coach speaking, rewrite it.

---

## 19. What to Avoid

- Comic Sans, Papyrus, novelty fonts — ever, anywhere
- Drop shadows on type
- Gradients on neon green
- Clipart, emojis in body, motivational stock posters
- More than 2 typefaces per page
- More than 3 levels of heading per page
- Walls of text with no whitespace
- Inconsistent margins across pages
- Low-contrast color pairings (light gray on white, dark gray on black)
- Mid-sentence emphasis with bold, italic, AND color all at once
- Mentioning specific cities, states, or local markets in athlete-facing copy

---

*End of style guide. When in doubt: simpler, sharper, blacker, fewer words, more whitespace.*
