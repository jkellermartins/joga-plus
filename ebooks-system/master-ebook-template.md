## 8. Training Rules

1. **Warm up every session.** No exceptions.
2. **Quality over quantity.** 20 focused minutes beats 60 distracted minutes.
3. **Both sides.** Train weak foot, weak hand, weak side — always.
4. **Slow then fast.** Master the movement, then add speed.
5. **No phones during reps.** Music yes, scrolling no.
6. **Finish what you start.** Complete every set.
7. **Track every session.** If it's not tracked, it didn't happen.
8. **Rest is training.** Sleep and hydration count.

---

## 9. JOGA+ Training Philosophy

JOGA+ blends **Brazilian creativity** with **structured academy methodology**. We believe great athletes are built — not born — through six pillars:

1. **Technical Development** — Clean, repeatable execution of fundamentals.
2. **Coordination & Body Control** — The body is the athlete's first tool.
3. **Creativity & Confidence** — Players solve problems, not memorize patterns.
4. **Game Intelligence** — Reading space, time, and opponents.
5. **Repetition With Purpose** — Every rep has a target. Mindless reps build mindless players.
6. **Long-Term Athlete Development** — We train players for ten years, not ten weeks.

Train the body. Train the mind. Train the player.
