Training Library FAQ

Common Questions.

Who are these guides for?

Youth athletes ages 8–18 (and motivated adults). Whether you’re new to a sport or playing competitively, the guides scale to your level. Parents can follow along too — many do.

Do I need special equipment?

No. A ball, sneakers, and a small space are enough for most drills. Anything optional (cones, agility ladder, wall) is called out clearly so you can substitute household items.

Can parents follow this?

Yes — the guides are written in plain language with step-by-step demos. Parents who’ve never coached have used them to run sessions with their kids.

How do I receive the ebook?

Instantly. After checkout you’ll get a PDF download link by email plus access to any video drill demos. Read on phone, tablet, laptop, or print it.
