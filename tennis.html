What Is Tennis Training at Joga+?

Joga+ offers private and group tennis lessons in Washington DC, Northern Virginia, and Maryland. Unlike traditional tennis academies, our program is designed as performance training — building lateral speed, reactive precision, and competitive discipline through court-based drills and live match scenarios. Our tennis coach, Diego Almeida, trained at the Guga Kuerten Academy in Brazil and is USPTA-certified.

Who Are Tennis Lessons For?

Complete beginners learning the sport for the first time. Junior tennis players building toward competitive tournament play. Multi-sport athletes who want court-based cross-training to develop lateral explosiveness and reaction speed. Youth athletes ages 8–18 across the DMV including Arlington, McLean, Fairfax, Bethesda, Falls Church, and Loudoun County.

Benefits of Private Tennis Coaching

01

Speed & Agility Development

Beginner tennis players gain footwork control. Advanced athletes develop split-second direction changes that translate to every surface.

02

Reactive Precision

Progress from reacting to anticipating. Builds the hand-eye coordination that makes athletes faster thinkers, not just faster movers.

03

Competitive Discipline

One-on-one with nowhere to hide. Builds the composure, self-reliance, and point-by-point focus that team sports can't.

04

Cross-Training Benefits

Sharpens lateral speed, reaction time, and racket coordination. Measurable advantages in your primary sport within the first month.

Tennis Training FAQ

Common Questions.

Do you offer private tennis lessons near me?

Yes. Joga+ offers private 1-on-1 tennis lessons across the DMV area, including Washington DC, Arlington, McLean, Falls Church, Fairfax, Bethesda, and Loudoun County. Our tennis coach, Diego Almeida, is USPTA-certified and trained at the Guga Kuerten Academy in Brazil. Private tennis lessons start at $299/month for 4 sessions, and we also offer group tennis training at $159/month. Your first session is free.

How much does tennis training cost?

Private tennis lessons are $299/month for 4 sessions. Group tennis training is $159/month. Tennis drop-in sessions are $35. The Elite program with 8 sessions per month is $499/month. All programs include filmed movement analysis and speed benchmarking. Your first session is free.

What ages do you train?

Joga+ tennis training is for youth athletes ages 8–18. Beginners learning the sport for the first time. Junior tennis players building toward competitive tournament play. Multi-sport athletes seeking court-based cross-training for lateral speed and reaction time.

Is tennis training good for beginners?

Yes. Many tennis athletes start as complete beginners. Tennis beginners develop footwork, racket control, and stroke fundamentals from day one. Our coach builds a development plan that matches every athlete's current level. There are no tryouts — just show up ready to learn.

Can tennis help my soccer or other sports?

Absolutely. Court training develops lateral speed, reaction time, and mental discipline. Soccer players see measurable improvements in transition speed and shuttle-run times. Multi-sport athletes develop hand-eye coordination and anticipation that transfers across every surface.

What happens after I sign up?

A Joga+ coach calls you within 24 hours to discuss your goals and schedule your first session. Your first training session is a free full assessment. You leave with a personalized plan based on real data, not a sales pitch. No contracts — cancel anytime.
