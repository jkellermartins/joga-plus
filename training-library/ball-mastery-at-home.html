Ball Mastery at Home.

A 4-week training guide for players ages 8–14 to improve control, touch, and confidence — in just 10 to 20 minutes a day, anywhere you can swing a foot at a ball.

Who This Is For.

  • Players ages 8–14
  • Beginners to intermediate
  • Players who want to improve outside of practice

What Players Will Improve.

  • First touch — receive any ball cleanly, under any pressure
  • Ball control — tight, deliberate, both feet
  • Coordination — foot speed, balance, and rhythm
  • Confidence on the ball — the willingness to take risks in tight spaces

What's Included.

  • 20+ drills with clear demos and progressions
  • 4-week structured plan that builds week by week
  • Daily routines — 10 to 20 minutes a day
  • Parent guidance — how to support without coaching
  • Progress tracking — benchmarks every 7 days

Equipment Needed.

  • 1 soccer ball
  • 4–6 cones (or shoes / water bottles work fine)
  • Small space — backyard, park, or garage

Inside the Guide.

Four weeks of structured progression. Each week builds on the last — from foundational ball familiarity to game-speed touches.
