Common Questions

Quick answers.

Is this guide good for beginners?
Yes. The 4-week plan starts with foundational ball familiarity and only adds complexity once the basics feel comfortable. A complete beginner can start Day 1 with no soccer background.
How long should players train per day?
10 to 20 minutes a day. Short, focused sessions beat long inconsistent ones — the guide is built around that principle.
Do I need a big space?
No. Every drill is designed for small spaces — a backyard, garage, hallway, or local park. A wall is helpful but not required.
How do I receive the guide?
Instantly. After purchase you'll get a download link by email plus access to QR-linked video drill demos. Read on phone, tablet, laptop, or print it.
