Local archive of submissions captured on this device.
How this works. Every submission to /apply is archived to this browser's localStorage (last 50 entries) — independent of the live backend. This page is a sanity check so you can verify the form is capturing data even before a backend is wired. Lead data only lives on the device that submitted it; production leads must still flow to your real backend (email, Google Sheets, Airtable, Supabase). Page is noindex.
