Frequently Asked Questions

Still Thinking?

What is the best age to start soccer training?

Children can start structured soccer training as early as age 8, when they develop the coordination and focus needed for skill development. At Joga+, we train youth athletes ages 8–18 at every level. Younger athletes (8–11) focus on fundamentals, ball mastery, and confidence. Older athletes (12–18) work on tactical awareness, position-specific skills, and competitive preparation for travel teams, ECNL, or D1 college recruitment.

Do you offer private tennis lessons near me?

Yes. Joga+ offers private 1-on-1 tennis lessons across the DMV area, including Washington DC, Arlington, McLean, Falls Church, Fairfax, Bethesda, and Loudoun County. Our tennis coach, Diego Almeida, is USPTA-certified and trained at the Guga Kuerten Academy in Brazil. Private tennis lessons start at $299/month for 4 sessions, and we also offer group tennis training at $159/month. Your first session is free.

What is footvolley training?

Footvolley (footvolley) is a Brazilian sport that combines soccer skills with beach volleyball rules. Players use their feet, chest, and head to volley a ball over a net on sand. Footvolley training builds explosive power, full-body coordination, and creative touch that transfers directly to soccer and other sports. Joga+ is the only academy in the DMV with CBFV-certified Brazilian footvolley coaches. No experience needed — beginners rally within their first sessions.

Can soccer players benefit from tennis or footvolley training?

Absolutely. Cross-surface training is the core of the Joga+ method. Sand training (footvolley) builds explosive power, balance, and body coordination that grass alone can't develop. Court training (tennis) develops lateral speed, reaction time, and mental discipline. ECNL parents in the DMV regularly report measurable improvements in their child's soccer performance after adding a second surface.

Is Joga+ good for beginners with no experience?

Yes. Many of our athletes start as complete beginners. Our coaches assess every new athlete individually and build a development plan that matches their current level. Beginners in soccer learn fundamentals and ball confidence. Footvolley beginners rally within their first few sessions. Tennis beginners develop footwork and racket control from day one. There are no tryouts — just show up ready to learn.
