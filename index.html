Joga+ Futvolley

Forged In Rio.
Elevated Here.

Futvolley isn't beach kickball. It's Brazil's fastest-growing competitive sport — a discipline that demands surgical touch, explosive athleticism, and the kind of creativity you can't teach in a gym. Joga+ brings it to the DMV at the highest level.

01

Brazilian Culture

Born on the beaches of Rio, futvolley carries the soul of Brazilian sport — creativity, rhythm, expression, and pure love for the game. At Joga+, we bring that authentic energy to every session.

02

Technical Mastery

Every touch matters over the net. We develop razor-sharp ball control with feet, chest, shoulders, and head. First touch, volleys, and setting become second nature.

03

Coordination & Fitness

Futvolley demands explosive agility, balance, and full-body coordination. Our training builds functional athleticism that transfers to any sport.

04

Fun & Community

The energy is different here. Music, sand, competition, and community. Futvolley builds friendships and a culture you actually want to be part of.

05

Premium Coaching

Our coaches have competed at the highest levels of futvolley in Brazil and internationally. Real experience, structured curriculum, measurable growth.

06

For All Levels

Never touched a futvolley net? Perfect. Already competing? Even better. We meet you where you are and push you to where you want to be.
