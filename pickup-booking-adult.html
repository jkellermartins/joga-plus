Adult Pickups at Joga+

Organized, competitive, community-driven games for adult players. Pick your sport, pick your slot, play.

Soccer Adult Soccer Pickup

  • Organized pickup format
  • Games are 5 minutes or 2 goals
  • Winners stay on, losers rotate
  • Up to 40 players per session
  • Balanced small-sided teams

Futvolley Adult Futvolley Pickup

  • Organized duo matches
  • Games up to 10 points
  • Usually more than one court running
  • Up to 14 players per session
  • Fast rotations, short rest
