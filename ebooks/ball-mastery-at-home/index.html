JOGA+ ACADEMY Week 01 · Foundations

Lesson 01.1

What is
ball mastery?

Ball mastery is the relationship a player has with the ball when nothing else is on their mind. It's not tricks. It's not flair. It's clean, confident touches with every surface of the foot — at any speed, in any direction, under any kind of pressure.

Why it matters

The best decision-makers on the pitch are the ones whose feet stop being a problem. Once the ball is no longer something to think about, the player's eyes come up and the game opens up.

How we train it

Short, daily, repeatable. Twenty minutes is the sweet spot — long enough for the body to learn, short enough for the brain to stay sharp. Every session in this guide follows that rule.

Coach's Note

A quiet touch is a confident touch. If your player's foot is slapping the ball, slow them down. Speed is a byproduct of control, not a replacement for it.

JOGA+ ACADEMY Week 01 · Drill 01

Drill 01 · Foundations

Inside-Inside
Touches.

Beginner 3 min Both Feet

How to perform

  1. Stand with the ball directly in front of you, feet shoulder-width apart.
  2. Tap the ball with the inside of your right foot to your left foot.
  3. Tap it back with the inside of your left foot to your right foot.
  4. Keep your touches small — the ball should never travel more than 6 inches.
  5. Stay on the balls of your feet. Knees soft. Eyes up after 30 seconds.
Coaching cue

"Quiet feet, fast feet." The slap of the ball should be barely audible. If it's loud, you're hitting too hard.

JOGA+ ACADEMY For Parents

For Parents

How to coach
without coaching.

Your job in these 20 minutes is not to teach technique. It's to protect the environment. Calm, focused, consistent. The drills do the coaching.

Do

Build the habit, not the highlight reel.
  • Set a consistent time and place. Same spot. Same start time.
  • Praise effort and quality of touches — not speed or tricks.
  • Let them finish frustrated sometimes. Frustration is data.
  • Record one drill per week on your phone for review.

Don't

Avoid the silent killers of motivation.
  • Compare them to teammates, siblings, or pros their age.
  • Re-explain the drill mid-rep. Let it land.
  • Extend the session as a reward — protect the 20-minute rule.
  • Treat missed days as failure. The plan absorbs missed days.
From the JOGA+ Staff

Players who finish this 4-week guide with a parent who simply showed up — phone away, on time, encouraging — outperform players whose parents tried to coach every rep. Presence beats instruction.
