JOGA+ ACADEMY Training Rules

The Standards

Training Rules.

Seven non-negotiables that turn home training into real development.

  1. Master control before speed. Speed only matters when the player can control the ball.
  2. Train both feet. The weak foot must be included every session.
  3. Keep the ball close. Small touches create control.
  4. Stay light on your feet. Good players move with balance and rhythm.
  5. Use your eyes. Look down when needed, but slowly learn to lift your head.
  6. Quality over quantity. Focused reps matter more than rushed reps.
  7. Be creative after control. Once the skill is clean, add rhythm, personality, and imagination.

Discipline creates freedom.

JOGA+ ACADEMY Warm-Up

Before Every Session

Warm-Up
Routine.

5–7 minutes before every session.

The warm-up prepares the body, activates coordination, and helps players find rhythm before technical work.

  1. Light Movement Jog, shuffle, skip, or move freely around the space.
    60 sec
  2. Dynamic Mobility Ankle rolls, hip circles, leg swings, and light lunges.
    90 sec
  3. Toe Taps Alternate feet on top of the ball with light touches.
    60 sec
  4. Foundation Touches Move the ball side to side using the inside of both feet.
    60 sec
  5. Sole Rolls Roll the ball across the body using the sole of the foot.
    60 sec
  6. Free Ball Movement Move freely with the ball using any touches, turns, or skills.
    60 sec
Coaching Points

Stay light on the feet · Keep the ball close · Bend the knees · Use both feet · Start slow and build rhythm

The warm-up is not extra. It is the first step of quality training.

JOGA+ ACADEMY Drill Library · Intro

Drill Library Intro

What is
ball mastery?

Ball mastery is the relationship a player has with the ball when nothing else is on their mind. It's not tricks. It's not flair. It's clean, confident touches with every surface of the foot — at any speed, in any direction, under any kind of pressure.

Why it matters

The best decision-makers on the pitch are the ones whose feet stop being a problem. Once the ball is no longer something to think about, the player's eyes come up and the game opens up.

How we train it

Short, daily, repeatable. Twenty minutes is the sweet spot — long enough for the body to learn, short enough for the brain to stay sharp. Every session in this guide follows that rule.

Coach's Note

A quiet touch is a confident touch. If your player's foot is slapping the ball, slow them down. Speed is a byproduct of control, not a replacement for it.

Drill 01 · Foundations

Toe Taps.

Beginner 3 × 30 sec Both Feet
Goal

Improve foot speed, rhythm, balance, and comfort touching the top of the ball.

Setup

One ball in a small space.

Instructions

  1. Place one foot lightly on top of the ball.
  2. Switch feet quickly while keeping the ball still.
  3. Stay on the balls of the feet and keep the knees slightly bent.
Start
Watch the Drill Scan for JOGA+ video demo QR_BMAH_01_Toe_Taps
Coaching Points
  • Light touches on top of the ball
  • Do not stomp
  • Keep chest up
  • Stay balanced
  • Build rhythm before speed
Common Mistakes
  • Hitting the ball too hard
  • Standing too upright
  • Looking down the entire time
  • Losing balance
Player Challenge

Complete 3 rounds without the ball rolling away.

Progression

Move the ball forward slowly while doing toe taps.

Drill 20 · Combination Flow

Turn Combination
Flow.

Intermediate 5 × 45 sec Creativity
Goal

Combine multiple turns and touches into a creative ball mastery flow.

Setup

One ball and four cones in a small grid.

Instructions

  1. Dribble freely inside the grid.
  2. Every 3–5 touches, perform a turn.
  3. Use pullbacks, inside cuts, outside cuts, and sole turns.
  4. Change direction after every turn.
  5. Add rhythm and creativity.
  6. Stay under control while increasing speed.

JOGA+ ACADEMY The Parent Role

Your Role

The Parent Role.

Parents do not need to coach every detail. The most important role is to create the environment, encourage consistency, and help the player enjoy the process.

A player improves faster when training feels supported, not pressured.

01 · EFFORT
Encourage Effort

Praise focus, attitude, and consistency more than perfect execution.

02 · ROUTINE
Create Routine

Help the player choose training days and protect those times each week.

03 · PROGRESS
Track Progress

Use the progress tracker to notice small improvements over time.

The goal is not to control every rep. The goal is to help the player build better habits.

jogaplusacademy.com The Parent Role · 40
JOGA+ ACADEMY Do · Avoid

Healthy Habits

Support the
Right Way.

Small habits from parents shape how players feel about training. Lead with patience and let the work do the teaching.

Do

What Parents Should Do
  • Encourage effort
  • Keep training positive
  • Help set a schedule
  • Celebrate small improvements
  • Record occasional videos for progress
  • Ask questions after training
  • Let the player make mistakes

Avoid

What Parents Should Avoid
  • Overcorrecting every mistake
  • Comparing the player to others
  • Making training feel like punishment
  • Focusing only on speed
  • Interrupting every rep
  • Showing frustration
  • Expecting instant results

Confidence grows when players feel safe to try, fail, adjust, and try again.
