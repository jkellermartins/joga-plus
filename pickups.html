Join organized Joga+ pickup sessions for soccer and futvolley. Competitive, fun, and community-driven games for kids and adults across the DMV.

Built for Players Who Want More

Joga+ pickups are a step above a casual kickaround. Every session is organized by Joga+ coaches, balanced by level, and run with real structure — so games stay sharp, teams stay fair, and everyone gets meaningful time on the ball. Show up, get matched, and play. Sessions run year-round across Washington DC, Northern Virginia, and Maryland, with pricing from $10 to $15 per session depending on format and location.

Two Sports. Two Age Groups. One Standard.

Every pickup is scheduled by age — Adults or Kids — and by sport: Soccer or Futvolley. Each format has its own rules, player cap, and rotation so games stay competitive and rest time stays short. Schedules rotate weekly. Reserve your spot before you head out.

Pickup FAQ

Common Questions.

How much do pickups cost?

Pickups run $10–$15 per session, depending on format and location. Drop-in pricing — no monthly commitment and no contracts.

What’s the soccer pickup format?

Organized pickup format. Games are 5 minutes or first to 2 goals — whichever comes first. Winners stay on, losers rotate. Up to 40 players per session, matched into balanced small-sided teams.

What’s the futvolley pickup format?

Organized duo matches, games up to 10 points. Usually more than one court running in parallel so rotations stay quick. Capped at 14 players per session.

Are adults and kids mixed together?

No. Every pickup is scheduled as Adults or Kids. Kids pickups are matched by age and level; adult pickups are open to all adult levels.

Do I need experience to join?

Not at all. Pickups are open to every level. Joga+ coaches balance teams on the spot so games stay competitive and fun no matter where you’re starting from.

How do I reserve a spot?

Spots are limited. Reserve through the booking page or message us directly on WhatsApp to confirm the next pickup near you.
