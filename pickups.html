Community Play

Joga+ Pickups
in Washington DC & Virginia.

Drop In. Play. Compete.

Joga+ pickups are open-play sessions for the DMV community — fast, competitive, and all levels welcome. Run by Joga+ coaches, every pickup keeps the same quality and rhythm you’d expect from a training session, just with more freedom on the ball. Show up, get matched into games, and play. Sessions run year-round across Washington DC, Northern Virginia, and Maryland. Pricing varies from $10 to $15 per session depending on format and location.

How Pickups Are Structured

Pickups are split by age group — Adults and Kids — and by sport: Soccer or Futvolley. Each session has its own format, player cap, and scoring rules so games stay sharp and everyone gets minutes on the ball. Session schedules rotate weekly. Confirm the next pickup before you head out.

Pickup FAQ

Common Questions.

How much do pickups cost?

Joga+ pickups run $10–$15 per session. Price varies by location, court rental, and format. Drop-in only — no monthly commitment.

What’s the soccer pickup format?

Games are 5 minutes or first to 2 goals — whichever comes first. Winners stay on, losers rotate. Up to 40 players per pickup, split into small-sided teams so everyone gets real touches.

What’s the futvolley pickup format?

Futvolley pickups are duo format, games up to 10 points. Usually more than one court running at the same time so rotations stay quick. Capped at 14 players per session.

Is there a split between adults and kids?

Yes. Every pickup is scheduled as Adults or Kids. Kids pickups are matched by age and level; adults are open to all adult levels.

Do I need experience?

No. Pickups are open to all levels. Joga+ coaches help balance teams so games stay competitive and fun no matter where you’re starting from.

How do I reserve a spot?

Spots are limited. Reserve through the booking page or message us on WhatsApp to confirm the next pickup near you.
