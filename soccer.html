What Is Soccer Training at Joga+?

Joga+ provides private and group soccer coaching for youth athletes in Washington DC, Northern Virginia, and Maryland. Our method is built on the Brazilian joga bonito philosophy — where technical skill, creative vision, and quick decision-making win games. Every session is position-specific, filmed for review, and benchmarked monthly so athletes and parents can see measurable improvement.

Who Is This For?

Youth soccer players ages 8–18 at any level. Beginners learning fundamentals. Travel and club athletes looking to earn a starting spot or make a higher-level team. ECNL and MLS NEXT players preparing for D1 college recruitment and showcases. Multi-sport athletes who want to sharpen their soccer skills. We serve families across Arlington, McLean, Bethesda, Fairfax, Falls Church, Loudoun County, and the greater DMV.

Benefits of Private Soccer Coaching

  • Position-specific development tailored to your game
  • Filmed sessions with tactical breakdown you can review at home
  • Monthly benchmark testing that tracks speed, touch, decision-making, and fitness
  • Brazilian-trained coaches with professional playing experience (Serie A, Primeira Liga, NWSL)
  • Small groups capped at 5 athletes, matched by ability level

Soccer Training FAQ

Common Questions.

What is the best age to start soccer training?

Children can start structured soccer training as early as age 8, when they develop the coordination and focus needed for skill development. At Joga+, we train youth athletes ages 8–18 at every level. Younger athletes (8–11) focus on fundamentals, ball mastery, and confidence. Older athletes (12–18) work on tactical awareness, position-specific skills, and competitive preparation for travel teams, ECNL, or D1 college recruitment.

How much does private soccer training cost?

At Joga+, private 1-on-1 soccer training starts at $299/month for 4 sessions ($75/session). Small group soccer training (3–5 athletes) is $159/month. Soccer skill clinics are $189/month. The Elite program with 8 sessions per month is $549/month. All programs include filmed sessions with tactical review and monthly benchmark testing. Your first session is always free.

Where can I train soccer near me in the DMV?

Joga+ serves youth soccer players across Washington DC, Northern Virginia, and Maryland. We train athletes from Arlington, McLean, Falls Church, Fairfax, Loudoun County, Bethesda, Rockville, and Silver Spring. Our coaches include former Serie A, Primeira Liga, and NWSL professionals with UEFA and USSF licenses. We offer both private and group soccer training with your first session free.

Do you offer group soccer training?

Yes. Small group soccer training is capped at 5 athletes and matched by age and skill level. Group soccer training is $159/month for 4 sessions. Sessions include competitive drills, rondos, live match scenarios, and weekly progress check-ins. Available across the DMV.

Is Joga+ good for beginners?

Yes. Many Joga+ athletes start as complete beginners. Coaches assess every new athlete individually and build a development plan matching their current level. Beginners learn fundamentals, ball confidence, and positional awareness from day one. There are no tryouts — just show up ready to learn. First session is free.

What happens after I sign up?

A Joga+ coach calls you within 24 hours to discuss your goals and schedule your first session. Your first training session is a free full assessment covering technical skill, physical ability, and game IQ. You leave with a personalized training plan based on real data, not a sales pitch. No contracts — cancel anytime.
